# DiwaliVerse 3D — Virtual Firecracker Store & Celebration Arena 🪔

A festive, high-performance 3D React + Three.js virtual celebration experience with 83 handcrafted procedural 3D firecrackers across 11 diverse categories, dynamic particle physics, spatial Web Audio synthesis, interactive 360° orbit camera, and a luxury Diwali UI.

100% Eco-Friendly • Zero Smoke • Pet & Family Safe • Pure Joy!

## 🎮 Interactive Features

- **Choreographed Show Playlist**: Add up to 6 crackers to your custom "Grand Finale Playlist" and fire them in an automated celebratory sequence with aerial camera tracking!
- **3D Cracker Lab**: Inspect each cracker in close-up with technical specifications, eco-badges, and studio test-firing.
- **Diwali Greeting Card Generator**: Personalize a festive Diwali greeting card with your name and custom message to share with family and friends.
- **Procedural 3D Models & Audio**: Real-time WebGL geometries, procedural canvas textures, and browser-synthesized Web Audio (fuse sizzle, rocket screamer, heavy blast, rapid ladi pops, meditative tanpura drone).
- **Click-to-Launch Sky**: Tap or click anywhere in the night sky to launch instant fireworks!
